# Name

Etsi ja löydä

# Based on Open Source English Song

https://nginx.leebasehome.com/music/songs/seek-and-find/

Open Source Album:

https://nginx.leebasehome.com/music/albums/swing-christmas-ii/

# Genres

swing, gospel

# Style

Big band swing, clipped staccato call-and-response drives a 160 BPM Christmas swing; walking upright bass, finger snaps, brushed swing drums shifting into breakbeat, brass stabs, trumpet solo, horn shout chorus and rising key-change finale; gritty, smooth male lead in a dark low register, clipped rhythmic phrasing, kids choir and gospel choir answers, close-miked with plate reverb.
