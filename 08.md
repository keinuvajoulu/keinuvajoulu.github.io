# Genres

swing, cinematic, pop

# Style

Big Band Swing with anthemic pop and funk-soul drive; full wall-of-sound production with a dramatic drum-roll build, gritty-smooth male vocals in rhythmic phrasing swelling into soulful belts, high-octane swing groove, heavy floor toms and breakbeat swing, electric bass, brass orchestra and trumpet, cinematic strings, atmospheric electronics, heavy guitars, virtuosic guitar-and-brass trade solo.
