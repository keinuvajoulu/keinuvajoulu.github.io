# Name

Joulukuun kaiut

# Genres

swing, cinematic, pop

# Style

Big Band Swing with anthemic pop, building breakbeat swing and high-octane pace; smooth, rhythmic male funk-soul vocals turning gritty and soulful; heavy floor toms, electric bass, brass orchestra, trumpet, cinematic strings, heavy guitars and atmospheric electronics, with a featured guitar-and-brass solo; full wall-of-sound production, dramatic drum roll and abrupt brass-hit finish.
