# Genres

swing, cinematic, pop

# Style

Big Band Swing with anthemic pop and funk-soul drive; gritty, smooth male vocals in rhythmic phrases, swelling into soulful belts; high-octane building swing groove with heavy floor toms and breakbeat swing, electric bass, brass orchestra and trumpet, cinematic strings, atmospheric electronics, and heavy guitars; virtuosic guitar-and-brass solo; dramatic drum-roll build into full wall-of-sound production.
