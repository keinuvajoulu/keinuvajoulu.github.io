# Style

Big band swing, anthemic pop, cinematic rock; gritty male vocals with a forceful spoken opening and choir-backed hook; tape-saturated rhythm section; heavy jungle drums, brass orchestra, heavy kick, hand claps, Hammond organ, bass, and guitar, with a frenetic call-and-response guitar and organ solo; high-energy swinging groove.
