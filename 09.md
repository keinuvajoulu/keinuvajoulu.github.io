# Genres

swing

# Style

Big band swing, piano, sleigh bells, glockenspiel, celesta, walking upright bass, brushed swing drums, bright brass and featured trumpet solo; 150 BPM upbeat swing, slow rubato piano-and-voice opening into full band, doo-wop backing vocals, key-change finale; low male lead in a dark register, gritty, smooth, warm, clipped rhythmic staccato phrasing.
